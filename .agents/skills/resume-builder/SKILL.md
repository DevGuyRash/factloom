# Resume builder

For a new batch, start with [research-mode onboarding](references/research-mode.md): proactively explain its enabled synthetic-content defaults and offer the controls and evidence-only choice before drafting. A user need not know the mode's name. Reuse existing batches' saved settings. Clear research/test context permits wholly generated or mixed career content under those controls; clarify ambiguous personal-career use before inventing claims. Evidence-only always disables invention, and genuine career work uses the process below. Keep the same files, commands, and build flow. Provenance, consent, observations, and real-person records remain factual; external testing still requires company agreement and the user's authorization.

This process serves any profession, seniority, and employment background. Derive priorities from this person’s target and evidence; no occupation or another person’s goals are defaults. A resume is evidence that this person can do the work the reader needs. Build the strongest supported case, not the longest inventory or highest keyword count. The repository keeps each claim in `people/<person>/resumes/source/facts.yaml`, its source in `evidence.md`, variants selecting facts, themes controlling appearance, and guides explaining targets ([resumes reference](../job-application/references/resumes.md)). For a new person, follow AGENTS.md's private-copy setup first.

## What holds throughout

- Capture the full usable career record before selecting a resume: preserve sanitized source detail, provenance, uncertainty, and corrections, then map it to reusable facts. Check what each rewrite retains or deliberately leaves out. Templates change presentation; they must carry the selected content faithfully.
- Facts come from the person and their authorized records. Research supplies selection criteria, vocabulary, and focused questions; typical duties stay proposed additions until the person's evidence supports them. A title, repository, or prototype alone does not establish duties performed, employment, production use, or results.
- New or changed factual meaning needs source verification and confirmation when not already approved. Preserve prior approval through purely editorial wording, shortening, or reordering that keeps the same factual scope and intended use. Unresolved bullet claims go in `confirm`; unresolved non-bullet claims go in the affected guide's manual `review`. Research changes emphasis and language, not qualifications.
- Read existing records before asking for more. Ask focused, material questions; draft the supported parts while uncertain claims wait. Review copies use `--out` so active files are preserved until the new version is approved. Resume work leaves application permissions and pauses unchanged.

## The work

1. **Frame the target.** Use the person's stated roles, level, field, locations, and constraints; resolve only material ambiguity. Record what they can do, for whom, and the proof. Ask once for a page-limit preference (a positive integer or use your judgment), recommending a default from the role, experience, market, and employer requirements; reuse an existing answer. A general resume can represent supported professional breadth without an exact vacancy. For a rewrite, establish what must carry over before shortening it.
2. **Research the decisions** ([research](references/research.md)). Read the exact role when supplied and adjacent industry roles that illuminate transferable strengths or real differences. Compare selection criteria with the person's evidence. Reuse dated sources; stop when more research no longer changes the case, variant boundaries, or material gaps. Record limits honestly.
3. **Recover missing proof** ([interviewing](references/interviewing.md)). Start with the records, then clarify relevant incidents, personal contribution, scale, users, and results. Record source and uncertainty as you go. Concrete work can be persuasive without metrics; never invent them.
4. **Write distinct variants** ([writing](references/writing.md)). Select the strongest evidence for each genuinely different audience while preserving supported search terms and skills. `./resumes variant new <name> --person <slug> --no-build` creates a guide and structure listing all facts; narrow it before rendering. Hold only variants whose selected content or intended use needs unresolved approval, not siblings using unchanged claims. Build review copies with `./resumes build-resumes --person <slug> --variant <name> --out <review-dir>`. The build's mechanical floor is neither editorial approval nor an ATS guarantee.
5. **Review and revise** ([review](references/review.md)). Have a fresh reader, when available, assess the built resume against the target before seeing the writer's rationale. Verify concerns against sources, inspect each delivered format's text and rendered pages, correct material defects, and check that corrections introduced none. Stop when remaining items need specific evidence or are optional preferences.
6. **Deliver and settle.** Give the person the reviewed files, each variant's purpose, material rewordings or omissions and why, and a focused batch of unresolved claims. Keep their theme unless a different layout is useful or requested; offer previews when appearance needs a decision. Update guides from the research rather than repeating it. Preserve review items until resolved.

A draft is deliverable with clearly labeled uncertainties. A variant is sendable when its factual content and intended use are covered by the person's approval, factual and manual review items are resolved, final active files pass review, and `./resumes check` passes (with any accepted mechanical warnings explained). Purely editorial revisions do not revoke that approval. Resume approval does not start applications. Report what was actually built and checked, and any unavailable format or blocked decision.
