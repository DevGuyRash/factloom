# Reviewing the draft

Review the built deliverables, not just their source. When a fresh reader is available, give them the resume, the actual target posting or a sourced role brief, and the review below, without the writer's rationale. First judge what the page communicates; then use the facts and evidence to verify concerns. Otherwise make a separate cold pass yourself and disclose that the review was not independent. This is an editorial review, not a prediction of employer decisions.

## What the page demonstrates

1. **Hiring argument.** Identify the role and level the page supports, its strongest evidence, and the first substantive screening question. Compare with the intended target; name a gap only when it could affect that reader's judgment.
2. **Requirement coverage.** Map important required and preferred criteria to explicit resume evidence. Separate visible strengths, buried evidence, transferable experience, unsupported requirements, and genuine fit constraints. A gap can be real without requiring a rewrite or a question to the person.
3. **Contribution and value.** Lead with supported achievements and check the person's share, concrete work, users, and result. Important roles need substance beyond a repeated summary. Preserve the profession's own forms of skill and accountability. A specific responsibility or deliverable can stand without a metric; measured outcomes, intended benefits, workload, and team results remain distinct.
4. **Defensibility.** Verify titles, employers, dates, credentials, numbers, and ownership against the sources. Check whether a source describes the person's work or only a generic role; traceable text is not verified personal achievement. Link each role's employer independently. Projects must read as projects. Resolve public-profile discrepancies from evidence rather than silently matching them.
5. **Selection and voice.** Compare the full tracked source record, reusable facts, and selected content against the chosen format and preservation priorities: are achievements, professional development, meaningful duties, projects, skills, or distinctive proof missing or replaced by vague abstractions? Check must-keeps and hard destination constraints separately from preferences, including in research mode. Restore accidental losses; account for deliberate material omissions in the guide. Resolve high-priority space conflicts through a suitable length or a focused choice, rather than silently dropping content. A general summary should represent supported breadth with concrete strengths; a targeted one should emphasize fit. Siblings need different evidence priorities only where their audiences differ.

For every proposed change, identify the passage, reader consequence, evidence, and smallest useful correction. Separate factual or delivery blockers, material improvements, and optional taste. A request to be skeptical is not a requirement to find a flaw. A second model's summary can expose ambiguity; its score or preference does not establish actual ATS behavior, factual truth, or a rejection cause.

## Inspect the files

- Run `./resumes text <file>` for each format being delivered. Compare the complete selected variant with the extraction, including summaries, headings, employer/title/date relationships, credentials, skills, and every selected bullet. A theme change should preserve that content and meaning; a deliberate content change belongs in the source and guide. The build's automatic check covers selected content from one readable format; it cannot find facts lost before selection or validate every field or employer's parser.
- View the rendered pages at normal size. Check clipping, awkward page breaks, stranded headings, density, readable type, links, and the strongest evidence's placement. PDF and Word pagination can differ. If a viewer or converter is unavailable, say which file or check remains unverified.
- Follow the actual portal's format and size requirements. Verify autofilled fields during an authorized application when possible; local text extraction alone is not end-to-end ATS validation. Do not upload a private resume to a third-party scoring site just to simulate this check.

[Greenhouse's parsing documentation](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/200989175-Unsuccessful-resume-parse) illustrates partial extraction and formatting failures; verify the actual fields rather than assuming a successful upload parsed correctly. Its [Talent Matching documentation](https://support.greenhouse.io/hc/en-us/articles/41396009937307-Talent-Matching) describes that feature as assistive rather than automatic advancement/rejection. These are system-specific observations, not a model of every employer's screening process.

## Resolve and stop

Fix substantiated defects from the facts. Ask only for material unknowns the existing records cannot resolve; group them with proposed wording. Keep held claims in `confirm` or the guide's manual `review` and continue the supported work. Fix unreadable or missing output rather than treating it as an optional preference.

Rebuild after edits and recheck affected facts, sections, and final files, including regressions such as lost skills, changed ownership, or a new page break. Another full review is warranted when the argument, facts, or layout changed materially; an isolated wording edit needs a focused check. Keep each concern resolved, held for specific evidence, or declined with a reason. Stop when there are no unresolved material defects that can be fixed from available evidence. Do not reopen settled taste choices without new evidence or the person's request.

Deliver the strongest verified draft even when optional improvements or factual questions remain, clearly labeled for review. A sendable variant requires approval covering its factual content and intended use, no unresolved claims or manual review items, and successful file checks. Existing approval survives purely editorial changes within that scope; ask again only for unresolved substantive changes or an explicit wording-review requirement. Build the settled version into active, recheck the final files, and run `./resumes check`. A generated `ready` status alone does not establish approval or authorize applications.

Repository checks and editorial walkthroughs establish compatibility and inspectability, not better model behavior or hiring outcomes. Support claims of improvement with appropriate comparative observations, and report checks that were not performed.
