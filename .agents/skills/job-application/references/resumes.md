# Resumes: sources, themes, building, keywords, tailoring

For reusable resume and web/profile summaries, use [named summary channels](../../../../docs/summaries.md). `facts.yaml` owns each channel's prose and primary fact references; variant sections use `summary: <id>` with optional `channel` (default `resume`). Inline paragraphs remain supported. Summary review is explicitly bound to wording and selected evidence; stale review and inherited source holds gate tailoring, without making review a truth certification. Export with `resumes summary show`, not the application-record export command.

The commands below serve genuine resumes and explicitly authorized [synthetic research](../../resume-builder/references/research-mode.md). Research controls govern generation and source use; the executor interprets them. The tools preserve declared artifact identity and guard synthetic submissions against the recorded bounded scope, not against invented consent. Genuine career and evidence-only content stays supported by evidence; a synthetic source remains synthetic even under an evidence-only override.

## Content, structure, and style are separate files

- Content: `people/<person>/resumes/source/facts.yaml` holds reusable sourced claims in `skills`, `projects`, `jobs`, and `lines`; the original sanitized record remains in imported notes and `evidence.md`. Bullets are `{text, keywords?, confirm?}`; `confirm` names phrases awaiting confirmation. Optional `sources: [...]` and `confirmed_by: [...]` retain source and confirmation references. Keep their attribution and dates; a reference does not clear an existing `confirm` hold until reconciliation resolves the claim. Never add unsupported genuine claims.
- Structure (which facts, in what order, under which headings): one file per variant in `people/<person>/resumes/source/variants/<variant>.yaml` — `headline`, `theme`, `output`, `sections` that reference facts by id (`labeled: [skill-id, ...]`, `entries: [{project|job: id, bullets?: [indexes], title?: override}]`, `lines: [line-id, ...]`, or a plain `paragraph`), and optionally `pages` (the most pages it may take) and `style` (theme settings for this variant only).
- Style: a theme (`./resumes themes list`). Themes are YAML files in `shared/templates/themes/` (the engine's), `custom/templates/themes/` (this repository's own), or `people/<person>/templates/themes/` (one person's); a theme can `extends:` another and change only what differs. `./resumes themes show <name>` prints every setting.

Change a resume by editing `facts.yaml` or a variant file, then rebuild. Never hand-edit the generated `.docx`/`.pdf`. Facts and variants can declare `mode: genuine_career|synthetic_research` and `batch_id`; tailoring persists that identity in the spec and build record. The canonical `resumes/source/research-controls.yaml`, when present, also supplies identity; generation switches and overrides remain agent-interpreted. Reconcile conflicting identities in the source rather than relabeling synthetic material as genuine.

## A person's first resume

1. Bring in their existing resumes ([intake](intake.md)), or, when they have none or theirs is not working, build one with the resume-builder skill (`./resumes import` reads `drop/` in any format; reading, reconciling versions, and writing `facts.yaml` are yours).
2. `facts.yaml` keeps the sourced claims. Preserve approved factual scope through editorial cleanup; unresolved new or changed assertions go in the bullet's `confirm`. See the builder's [writing](../../resume-builder/references/writing.md) for the distinction between meaning and wording.
3. `./resumes variant new <name> --person <slug> --no-build [--theme <theme>]` creates a guide and structure listing every fact; select and order them for the purpose and established preservation priorities. Hold only variants selecting unresolved claims or requiring approval of a new intended use; an unused changed bullet does not hold a sibling. Use `build-resumes --person <slug> --variant <name>` directly for approved changes, adding `--out <review-dir>` while substantive approval is pending; review copies do not regenerate guide flags. Use `--from <variant>` only when another target needs a different argument or evidence selection.
4. Follow the builder's [source-to-final review](../../resume-builder/references/review.md): compare original evidence and user corrections through facts and selection to the delivered text, inspect the actual diff and each format's pages, and explain material omissions against the purpose. Settle affected guide `review` items before use; non-bullet claims need manual items.
5. Research the market for each variant and write its guide (below).

## Writing and refreshing a guide

A guide says what its resume is for: suitable roles, boundaries with sibling variants, and how to find them. Create or refresh it when the target changes or research could change a writing or fit decision. A missing or old `researched` date in `./resumes status` prompts a relevance check; reuse findings that still support the decision instead of repeating the research.

1. Follow the resume-builder [research](../../resume-builder/references/research.md): reuse current findings, inspect distinct employers' actual requirements, map them to evidence, and cover differences that could change the target or writing. Record source dates, coverage, limits, and material gaps. Research pay when it affects the target or the person asks; do not repeat a fixed posting quota for every sibling variant.
2. Decide the targets from that research: the titles employers use for this work today, newer titles included; the seniority the person's facts support; the industries and kinds of employers hiring; and the qualifications postings commonly require. Keep only targets the person's facts support, and list stretch roles apart for the person to approve.
3. Write the guide: `headline`, `use_for` (role families, best fits first), `avoid_for` (naming the sibling variant to use instead), `keywords` (terms supported by the person's facts), "Target roles" (titles, seniority, industries, and relevant sourced pay), and dated "Market notes" with the evidence mapping and sources. Set `researched` to the date findings were actually checked; disclose limited coverage. Consider whether an engagement constraint belongs in the headline or guide for the intended audience.
4. When job-search setup is in scope, save searches for the supported target titles on the person's job sites, sorted newest first (`./resumes searches add <id> --url <url> --variant <variant> --site <site> --query <title>`), and remove obsolete targets. Guide research by itself does not start searches, applications, or schedules, or change `apply`.
5. Show the person the targets and the stretch roles; anything they question goes in the guide's `review`.

Research shapes the targeting only. It never adds a claim to a resume: a qualification the market wants that the person's facts do not show is a gap to tell them about, never a keyword to add.

## Choosing a theme

- `./resumes themes preview --person <slug> --variant <name> --png` renders the variant in every theme for the person to choose from, with page counts.
- Use a theme marked `ats: safe` as the starting point for portals, then verify the delivered file's text and layout; the label is not certification by an employer's parser. A theme marked `ats: caution` (the sidebar layout) is for a person-first context. `./resumes build-resumes --theme <name> --out <dir>` renders a one-off copy without changing the variant.
- When a variant must stay within a page count, set `pages: N`; the build tightens spacing, type (never below 9 pt), and margins, and reports changes. Prefer better content selection over unreadable compression; the floor is not the target.
- `./resumes themes new <name> --from <theme>` starts a theme in `custom/` (or the person's own with `--person`); `./resumes themes check` reports unreadable colors and fonts this machine lacks.

## Building

`resumes build-resumes [--person <slug>] [--variant <name>] [--theme <name> --out <dir>] [--out <dir>]` renders every variant under `resumes/source/variants/` (or just `--variant`) into `people/<person>/resumes/active/<variant>/<output>.docx` (+ `.pdf` when LibreOffice is available), or under `--out` when given. `resumes build-resumes --check` writes nothing: it lists generated resumes whose rebuild would differ from their files (`resumes update` runs it after bringing in a new engine).

## Review-item derivation

After each variant builds into its active directory, its `guide.md` `review` and `status` fields are rewritten from the `confirm` phrases of the bullets that variant actually uses (`status: needs-review` when any remain, `ready` otherwise), keeping review items the person wrote by hand; `generated: true` records that the files come from `resumes/source/`, and `fitted` records the page-fitting step a variant with `pages` needed, so checks reproduce the file without LibreOffice. Edit `confirm` lists in `facts.yaml`, not the guide, and rebuild.

Keep unresolved non-bullet claims and necessary pending approval as manual `review` items, with `status: needs-review`. Preserve approval for purely editorial changes within its scope and leave unchanged sibling variants alone. `ready` means recorded flags are clear; it does not prove factual truth, approval, visual quality, or actual ATS parsing.

## Keywords

`resumes keywords <application-dir> [--variant <name>]` compares the posting with the chosen material through `shared/lexicon.yaml` plus `custom/lexicon.yaml`, writing `keywords.md` (`type: keyword-report`). Use `--artifact <file.docx|pdf>` for actual final bytes and `--spec <tailored-yaml>` for the relevant spec or revision; `--source <original.md|txt|docx|pdf>` supplies original evidence (default `evidence.md`), and `--baseline <prior.docx|pdf>` supplies the actual text review diff. `--omit <fact-id=reason>` records a justified relevant omission. Reports separate confirmed-source lexical matches, awaiting confirmation, source-only leads, unknown evidence, and relevant omissions, including omitted fact ids whose keywords appear elsewhere. With submission history, the default requires the recorded selected upload and compares its frozen bytes; retained candidates alone do not establish which file was sent. Missing historical bytes or selection remain unknown. Explicit `--variant` requests a current-source projection, not proof of what was sent. Add vocabulary to `custom/lexicon.yaml`, not the command.

This is a lexical diagnostic, not a qualification or hiring score. Review required and preferred responsibilities against candidate evidence and its confidence, separating confirmed support, unresolved claims, and supported material omitted from the selected variant. Source-only lines are leads to reconcile, not confirmed qualifications; `sources` or `confirmed_by` references do not override a held `confirm`. Inspect original evidence and user corrections before changing content. Omit irrelevant facts or record a justified material omission rather than forcing the whole inventory into every output.

## Tailoring

`resumes tailor <application-dir> [--variant <name>] [--theme <name>] [--allow-unconfirmed] [--revision <new-name>]` starts from the selected variant, reorders its entries, bullets, and skill groups toward posting keywords, and writes `resume-tailored.yaml` plus fitted `.docx`/`.pdf` files and a build record. It also generates `keywords.md` against the actual rendered output, surfacing relevant omitted facts. After sent, possible-click, or outcome history, use `--revision <new-name>` for a separate draft in `revisions/<new-name>/`. Edit its spec and use `--spec --revision <name>` to rebuild that unsent draft. Earlier artifacts and submission associations stay unchanged. Unconfirmed `confirm` phrases remain blocked unless `--allow-unconfirmed` requests a review preview.

Check the selected and source-wording guides yourself: the command's bullet check does not enforce manual review items. Use unresolved material only in a clearly labeled review preview; `--allow-unconfirmed` never makes it sendable.

The keyword order is a starting point. Compare the target's required/preferred work with the full facts and original evidence, including supported facts omitted from the chosen variant. Use confirmation status and source pointers to decide what can be surfaced, what needs evidence, and what is irrelevant; no term match proves qualification. Edit `resume-tailored.yaml` to bring in supported fact references, drop irrelevant material, reorder, or use a sibling's settled headline, then render with `resumes tailor <application-dir> --spec`. The command accepts headlines, paragraphs, and entry titles already present in variants. Put new wording in a variant or `facts.yaml` first; confirm only factual meaning or intended use beyond existing approval. Review the actual diff and final extraction against original evidence, purpose, and user corrections using the builder's [review](../../resume-builder/references/review.md). Preserve an already-sent artifact and its identity before making a later revision.
